# Section 13: Applied Robotics — MoveIt 2 and Robot Manipulation

[Back to course index](../README.md) · [Practical commands and checkpoints](../applied-practical-checkpoints.md)

## Lecture 74: MoveIt 2 Introduction

Understand the role of MoveIt 2 in motion planning and manipulation. Review robot models, planning groups, collision checking, robot state, trajectories, controllers, RViz tools, and the Move Group architecture.

## Lecture 75: Inverse Kinematics: IK Basics

Understand how inverse kinematics converts a desired end-effector pose into possible joint configurations. Review multiple solutions, unreachable targets, joint limits, singularities, and solver behavior.

## Lecture 76: Forward Kinematics: FK Concept

Compute an end-effector pose from known joint values and the robot's kinematic chain. Relate link transforms, joint motion, and the accumulated base-to-tool transform.

## Lecture 77: MoveIt 2 Planning Pipeline

Follow a planning request through constraints, inverse kinematics, collision checking, the planning plugin, time parameterization, trajectory validation, and execution. Understand where common planning failures originate.
